1. 我们收集什么 / What we collect

排盘需要的信息：姓名（可不填真名）、性别、出生日期、出生时间、出生地（可选）。免费排盘在您的浏览器本地计算，默认不上传到我们的服务器。付费 AI 解读时，系统会把命盘摘要（宫位、星曜、四化、大限，不含您的姓名以外的身份信息）发送给 AI 服务商用于生成解读，生成后不长期保存您的出生信息。

To generate a chart we need your name (a nickname is fine), sex, birth date, birth time and optionally birthplace. Free charting is computed in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers by default. For a paid AI reading, a chart summary (palaces, stars, Si Hua and Da Xian, without identity information other than the name you entered) is sent to our AI provider to generate the reading, and your birth data is not stored long-term after generation.

2. 我们如何使用 / How we use it

仅用于生成您请求的命盘与解读、处理付款与防滥用（按 IP 与设备计免费次数与限流）。我们不会把您的出生信息出售、出租或分享给第三方用于广告。

We use this data only to generate the chart and reading you requested, to process payment, and to prevent abuse (free-trial counts and rate limits by IP/device). We do not sell, rent or share your birth data with third parties for advertising.

3. 付款信息 / Payment data

付款由第三方支付服务处理，您的银行卡、支付宝或微信支付信息直接提供给支付服务商，不会经过或存储在本站服务器上。本站只收到付款是否成功的结果。

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. Your card, Alipay or WeChat Pay details go directly to that provider and are not stored on our servers. We only receive whether the payment succeeded.

4. 本地存储 / Local storage

本站可能在您的浏览器本地保存最近一张命盘、您是否用过免费试读，以及您在 AI 页签里自行填写的 API 密钥（仅用于您自己浏览器直接调用 AI 时，密钥只发给 AI 服务商，不发给我们）。清除浏览器数据即可删除。

The site may store your most recent chart, whether you have used the free trial, and any API key you choose to enter in the AI tab (used only by your browser to call the AI provider directly; it is not sent to us) in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

5. 您的权利 / Your rights

您可以随时停止使用本站、清除本地数据，或发邮件到 wuuyboo@gmail.com 要求删除我们可能保存的与您相关的记录。我们会在合理时间内处理。

You may stop using the site at any time, clear local data, or email wuuyboo@gmail.com to ask us to delete any records we may hold about you. We will handle such requests within a reasonable time.

6. 未成年人 / Minors

本站不面向 18 岁以下用户提供付费服务。未成年人请在监护人同意下使用免费排盘。

Paid services are not offered to users under 18. Minors should use the free charting only with a guardian's consent.

7. 政策变更 / Changes

我们可能更新本政策，更新后在本站公布并以公布日期为准。
